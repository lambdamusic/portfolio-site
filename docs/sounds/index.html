Sounds / music written as code

I make music by writing code and changing it while it plays, mostly with Extempore and Ableton Live. Below are some of the performances, two old albums and a few one-off tracks. New pieces go up on The Musical Code.

Livecoding 16

Techno jam 2024 · This piece plunges into a heavy, atmospheric soundscape driven by the collision of overlapping basslines and raw industrial noise. Ping pong delays 2024 · This livecoding piece crafts a suspenseful and ambient sonic environment built entirely from mallet percussion. Out of this world (2024-02) 2024 · This generative ambient piece is a direct sonic meditation on the philosophy of Alan Watts, specifically his idea that "you don't come into this world, but you come out of this world." Dreamy pianos - Study No 2 in B Minor 2024 · Hypnotic piano chords and melodies are layered and phased using cosine oscillators, creating an immersive, wave-like sonic space of infinite, shifting sound. Dreamy pianos - Study No 1 in C Minor 2023 · An experiment using overlapping, cosine-driven piano lines in C minor to demonstrate how simple phasing techniques can produce surprisingly complex and immersive musical effects. Exploring Bento noise box 2022 · Improvised acid loops using Extempore + Bentō. Study for Cello and Double-bass 2022 · This piece draws inspiration from the epic, thematic drama of Game of Thrones, featuring a rich texture built from intertwining cello melodic lines. Rhythmic Cycles 2021 · "Rhythmic Cycles" is a new livecoding composition created using Extempore and Ableton Live. Piano Scales 2020 · "Piano Scales" is a new experiment in algorithmic composition, demonstrating the captivating simplicity of piano-based systems. New livecoding screencast: Ziggurat 51 2013 · So hard to find time to do something creative these days. Livecoding 'Fjords' in Paris & Strasbourg 2011 · New song called 'Fjords': ambient electronica scales on a rollercoaster. Turborobot 2010 · "Turborobot" is a happy, high-energy electronica piece with a distinct electro-disco vibe that throws back to the 80s. Livecoding Xmas event @ Goldsmith College, London 2010 · Recording from Thursday Club Xmas party at Goldsmiths college, London. Livecoding session @ Anatomy Theatre, King's College, London 2010 · "Untitled 12" is an electronic music experiment performed via livecoding at the Anatomy Theatre, King's College, London. Xanadu 2010 · A new experiment, "Xanadu". Requires patience as the complex song base builds before revealing its most interesting and evolving sonic layers. Kali (an experiment with Impromptu) 2009 · An ambient musical experiment created with Impromptu, designed to transport the listener to Kali, a serene Japanese musical garden.
