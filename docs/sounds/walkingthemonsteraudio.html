Walking the Monster

A short electronic piece, created as a soundtrack for the Walking the Monster video.

I've made this short clip trying to match the moving images from CarolCat in the "Walking the monster" video.
It's a piece that alternates a quite repetitive and hypnotic theme (=the night), with a happier and enthusiastic one (=the day).

Made with Propellerheads Reason.
