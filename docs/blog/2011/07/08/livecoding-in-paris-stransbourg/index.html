New song called 'Fjords': ambient electronica scales on a rollercoaster. This is a recording from a workshop and gig at the 2011 Libre Software Meeting in Strasbourg (event page | Facebook | Last.fm) on July 7th, 2011.

Two live coding concerts coming up in the next few days:

  • 9/7/11: A performance in Paris at La Générale, "Laboratoire artistique, politique et social". 14, avenue Parmentier, Paris XIe, Métro Voltaire (Facebook | Last.fm)
  • 11/7/11: A workshop and performance at the 2011 Libre Software Meeting in Strasbourg (event page | Facebook | Last.fm)
