Academic Analytics Reports

Analytics reports for the academic and publishing sectors

Years
2021
Status
Completed
Role
Design and development

A collection of research intelligence reports focusing on the scholarly publishing and academic domains. The reports offer insights into several key areas, such as:

  • Performance statistics for publications
  • Competitors analysis
  • Collaboration patterns
  • Funding

The reports are generated dynamically from journals or organizations identifiers. Built using Google Data Studio, Dimensions on BigQuery and other custom web components.

Technology

  • Google Data Studio for the reports
  • Dimensions on Google BigQuery for data
